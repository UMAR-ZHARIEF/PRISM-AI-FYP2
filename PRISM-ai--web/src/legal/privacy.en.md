# PRISM-AI Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0.0 — Effective from 19 May 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how PRISM-AI collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data, in compliance with the Malaysian **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709)** as amended by the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act A1727)**.

If you are reading this on behalf of a child, please note that this policy treats children's data with extra protection because Malaysian law treats minors as unable to consent on their own [Contracts Act 1950, Section 11].

---

## 2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

### About pupils
- Full name, student number, year, class
- Date of birth, gender
- Profile photo (if uploaded)
- **Face embedding** — a list of numbers that represents the geometric structure of the child's face, generated by an AI model. *This is sensitive personal data.* See Section 8 below.
- Attendance records (date, time, status: present/absent/late)
- Teacher notes about behaviour, performance, or welfare

### About parents and staff
- Full name, email, phone number
- Role in the system (parent, teacher, admin, assistant)
- Profile photo (if uploaded)
- Records of which Terms and Privacy Policy versions you have accepted
- Records of any consents you have given or withdrawn for your child's biometric data
- Audit log entries when you take significant actions (login, edits, deletions)
- IP address and browser user-agent (for security and audit)

We collect data from:

- **You**, when you fill in forms in PRISM-AI.
- **The school**, when they enrol your child.
- **Automatic devices** in the school (camera at the entrance) when face recognition is enabled with consent.

## 3. Why we collect it

We use personal data only for these purposes:

1. **Attendance**: marking the child present, absent, or late.
2. **Notifications**: alerting parents about their child's attendance and important school events.
3. **Reports**: generating statistics for the school principal, teachers, and PIBG.
4. **Security and audit**: detecting misuse, investigating disputes, complying with legal duties.
5. **Compliance**: keeping the records required by Malaysian education and data-protection law.

We do **not** use personal data for:

- Marketing or advertising.
- Selling or renting to third parties.
- Training AI models outside of the school's own attendance use, unless separate explicit consent is given.

## 4. Legal basis

We rely on the following lawful bases under PDPA 2010:

- **Consent**, obtained from the parent or legal guardian for biometric data [Section 40 — explicit consent for sensitive personal data].
- **Performance of a contract**, for non-biometric data needed to operate the school enrolment.
- **Legal duty**, where Malaysian law requires us to keep certain records (e.g., Education Act 1996).

## 5. Who we share data with

We share personal data **only** with:

- **School staff** who need the data to do their job (your child's teacher, the school admin).
- **Ministry of Education (KPM/APDM)** where Malaysian law requires the school to report student data.
- **Our cloud processor (Supabase)** which hosts the database. Supabase processes the data only under our written instructions.
- **Law enforcement or courts**, only when validly ordered.

We do **not** share data with marketing companies, social media platforms, or any party not listed above.

## 6. Where data is stored

Personal data is stored in:

- A database hosted on **Supabase**, with servers located in a region selected by the school (default: closest region to Malaysia).
- A local file on the school's camera device for face embeddings during attendance taking.

### Cross-border transfer

Some hosting infrastructure may be located outside Malaysia. Under PDPA Section 129 (as amended in 2024), we ensure such transfers either:

- Are to a country with similar data-protection laws, or
- Are protected by contractual safeguards with our processor (Supabase has Data Processing Agreements that include these safeguards), or
- Are with your explicit consent.

You will be told before any new cross-border transfer if it has not already been disclosed.

## 7. How long we keep data

We keep data only as long as we need it:

| Type of data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Pupil records (name, year, class) | While the pupil is enrolled, plus 7 years after they leave (Education Act archival rules) |
| Attendance records | 7 years (admissibility under Evidence Act 1950 Sections 90A-C) |
| Face embeddings | Deleted within 90 days of the pupil leaving the school, OR consent withdrawal, OR end of academic year — whichever is earliest |
| Notes by teachers | While the pupil is enrolled, plus 1 year |
| Audit logs | 3 years |
| Notifications | 1 year, then archived |

You can ask us to delete personal data sooner — see "Your rights" below.

## 8. Special rules for biometric data (face)

Face data is classified as **sensitive personal data** under PDPA Section 4, after the 2024 amendments [Act A1727, Section 3(c)].

We process face data **only** if:

1. The parent or legal guardian has given **explicit written consent** [PDPA Section 40], using the [Biometric Data Consent Notice](/biometric-consent).
2. The pupil has a non-biometric alternative if the parent refuses (manual roll call). **Refusing consent does not affect the child's right to attend school.**
3. The embedding is stored as a list of numbers, not as a photograph.

You can **withdraw consent at any time** through the Parent Portal. We will delete the embedding within 7 calendar days of withdrawal.

## 9. Your rights

Under PDPA 2010 (as amended), you have the right to:

- **Access** — ask us what data we hold about you and your child [Section 30].
- **Correct** — ask us to fix mistakes [Section 34].
- **Withdraw consent** — for any purpose where consent is the basis [Section 38].
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format and ask us to send it to another controller [new right under 2024 amendments].
- **Object** — to processing that causes you damage or distress.
- **Complain** — to the school's DPO, or to the Department of Personal Data Protection at https://www.pdp.gov.my/.

You can exercise these rights through:

- The **"My Data"** screen in the Parent Portal.
- Email to the school's DPO (Section 1 above).
- A written letter to the school office.

We will respond within **21 days** of receiving your request.

## 10. How we protect data

We follow the PDPA Standard 2015 (security, retention, integrity):

- All personal data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Face embeddings are encrypted at rest.
- Access is restricted to authorised users by role.
- Every access to face data is recorded in an audit log.
- We test our security at least annually.
- We have an incident response plan ready (see Section 11).

## 11. If there is a data breach

If personal data is accidentally disclosed, lost, altered, or accessed without permission, we will:

1. **Notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner** within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach, as required by PDP Commissioner Circular No. 2/2025 (in force 1 June 2025).
2. **Notify affected parents and staff** without unnecessary delay, when there is risk of significant harm.
3. **Investigate** the cause and take steps to prevent recurrence.
4. **Record** the breach in our internal register for future audit.

You will receive a written notice (email and/or letter) telling you what happened, what data was affected, what we are doing, and what you should do (e.g., change your password).

## 12. Cookies and similar technology

PRISM-AI uses a small amount of local browser storage to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics that share data with marketing platforms.

## 13. Children

PRISM-AI processes data **about** children but does not give children their own accounts. Parents access information on the child's behalf.

We follow the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 16 (privacy of the child), which Malaysia ratified in 1995 and has not reserved.
