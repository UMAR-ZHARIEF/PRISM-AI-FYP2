## 1. Who can use PRISM-AI

PRISM-AI accounts are issued by the school. Only the following people may use the system:

- **Administrators** appointed by the school principal.
- **Teachers and assistants** employed by the school.
- **Parents or legal guardians** of enrolled pupils.

**Children do not have their own accounts.** Parents access information on the child's behalf. This is required by Malaysian law because children below 18 cannot give legally binding consent on their own [Contracts Act 1950, Sections 10-11; Age of Majority Act 1971].

You must be at least 18 years old to register or accept these Terms.

## 2. What PRISM-AI does

PRISM-AI helps the school:

- Record daily attendance, including by automatic face recognition where consent has been given.
- Generate reports for school administration.
- Send notifications to parents about their child's attendance.
- Maintain a roster of pupils, classes, teachers, and subjects.

PRISM-AI is **not** a substitute for legally required school records held by the Ministry of Education (KPM/APDM). The school remains responsible for those.
